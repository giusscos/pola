## Description *(4000)*
```
Poly turns your iPhone into an instant film camera.

Press the shutter and watch your shot slide out and slowly develop, just like the real thing. Shake your phone to speed it up. Add a handwritten caption, flip the print over to see where it was taken, and keep every shot in a library that feels like a shoebox of prints.

13 FILM STOCKS
From warm golden-hour tones and faded 70s sepia to lavender instant prints, infrared, thermal, cyanotype, night vision, RGB glitch and VHS horror. Preview any stock live in the viewfinder before you shoot.

VINTAGE LENSES
Put a cheap plastic lens, coarse grain or a worn toy camera with light leaks on top of any film stock.

INSTANT FORMATS
Shoot Classic, Square, Wide or Mini. The viewfinder shows exactly what will end up in the print. Pick any frame color, or match it to your film.

MEMORIES WIDGET
A photo from this day in past years, right on your Home Screen. No anniversary today? It brings back another favorite from your library.

MAKE IT YOURS
Captions in 6 fonts and 4 weights, a longer note on the back, and a retro orange date stamp like the compact cameras of the 90s.

VIDEO & TIME LAPSE
Record moving prints, or set an interval and turn a whole afternoon into a single time lapse.

DOUBLE EXPOSURE
Layer two shots on a single print. A ghost of the first shot helps you line up the second, and a slider balances the two, from dreamy to bold.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
Unfold for a big viewfinder with every control right beside it. The shutter steers clear of the fold, and Poly adapts to every way you hold it.

SHARE & PRINT
Save clean prints to Photos, share a 9:16 image made for Stories, or lay out A4 print sheets with cut marks and print them at home.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your photos stay in your library and your iCloud, synced across your devices.

POLY PREMIUM
Free forever: the camera, video, time lapse, the SOLVA film stock and frame color, and your whole library.
Premium unlocks every film stock, lens, frame color and format, double exposure, caption fonts, the date stamp, the Memories widget, print sheets, colorful app icons and logo-free exports. Choose monthly, yearly (new subscribers can start with a free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Terms of Use: https://poly-vintage.com/terms
Privacy Policy: https://poly-vintage.com/privacy
```
> If the free trial isn't live yet, delete "(new subscribers can start with a free trial)".
