## Description *(4000)*
```
Poly is an instant film camera app for iPhone. Every photo slides out as a print and develops in your hands, with 17 film stocks, 3 vintage lenses and Classic, Square, Wide and Mini formats. It's for anyone who loves the look of instant film without buying film packs. Free to use; Premium unlocks every look.

AT A GLANCE
• Free: the camera, video, time lapse, importing your photos, the SOLVA film stock and frame color, and your whole library
• Price in euro countries: €2.99 a month, €19.99 a year or €29.99 once; other countries see their local price in the app
• New subscribers can try the yearly plan free for 7 days
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your photos stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud, synced across your devices
• In English, German, Spanish, French and Italian

Shake your phone to develop it faster, add a handwritten caption, flip the print to see where it was taken, and keep every shot in a library that feels like a shoebox of prints.

17 FILM STOCKS
From warm golden-hour tones and faded 70s sepia to lavender instant prints, expired film, night film with a red halation glow, wet-plate tintype, newsprint halftone, infrared, thermal, cyanotype, night vision, RGB glitch and VHS horror. Preview any stock live in the viewfinder before you shoot.

BRING YOUR OWN PHOTOS
Import any photo from your library, give it a film stock, lens and frame, and watch it print and develop. It keeps the day and place it was taken.

A REAL CAMERA
Tap to focus, pinch to zoom, use the flash, or take the shot with the volume buttons.

VINTAGE LENSES
Put a cheap plastic lens, coarse grain or a worn toy camera with light leaks on top of any film stock.

INSTANT FORMATS
Shoot Classic, Square, Wide or Mini. The viewfinder shows exactly what will end up in the print. Pick any frame color, or match it to your film.

MEMORIES WIDGET
A photo from this day in past years, right on your Home Screen. No anniversary today? It brings back another favorite from your library.

MAKE IT YOURS
Captions in 6 fonts and 4 weights, a longer note on the back, and a retro orange date stamp like the compact cameras of the 90s.

VIDEO & TIME LAPSE
Record moving prints, or set an interval and turn a whole afternoon into a single time lapse.

DOUBLE EXPOSURE
Layer two shots on a single print. A ghost of the first shot helps you line up the second, and a slider balances the two, from dreamy to bold.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
Unfold for a big viewfinder with every control right beside it. The shutter steers clear of the fold, and Poly adapts to every way you hold it.

SHARE & PRINT
Save clean prints to Photos, share a 9:16 image made for Stories, or lay out A4 print sheets with cut marks and print them at home.

POLY PREMIUM
Free forever: the camera, video, time lapse, importing your photos, the SOLVA film stock and frame color, and your whole library.
Premium unlocks every film stock, lens, frame color and format, double exposure, caption fonts, the date stamp, the Memories widget, print sheets, colorful app icons and logo-free exports. Choose monthly, yearly (new subscribers can start with a free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Terms of Use: https://poly-vintage.com/terms
Privacy Policy: https://poly-vintage.com/privacy
```
> Check the euro prices in App Store Connect → Pricing before pasting. If the free trial isn't live, delete the trial line under the opening facts and the free-trial words in the Premium section.
