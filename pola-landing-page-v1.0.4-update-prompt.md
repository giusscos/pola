## Core Features Section — Why Download Today

Keep these — they're strong conversion drivers. One icon + headline + one sentence each.

- **10 film filters** — 5 classic emulations (warm, cool, B&W, faded Polaroid) plus the new Weird Film pack. Every look is crafted, not a generic preset.
- **Colored instant packs** — Match your mood with iconic frame colors. Your polaroids feel personal from the first shot.
- **Photo, video & time-lapse** — One app, three ways to capture memories with the same vintage character.
- **Location on the back** — Flip any polaroid to see where it was taken. Memories tied to places, just like writing on the white border.
- **Your signature caption** — 6 fonts, 4 weights. Handwrite a note on every shot.
- **Real-time preview** — See the filter before you shoot. No surprises.
- **iCloud sync** — Your polaroids follow you across your devices.
- **Clean exports** — Share pure polaroids, no clutter (Premium).
- **Available in 5 languages** — English, German, Spanish, French, Italian.

**Avoid:** API names, "SwiftData," "AVFoundation," deployment targets, or anything a user wouldn't care about before downloading.

## Copy Rules

- Headlines: under 8 words
- Body: under 20 words per sentence
- Lead with emotion ("relive," "capture," "develop," "remember") before features
- Name filters sparingly in marketing copy — use evocative descriptions ("thermal heat," "horror VHS") rather than internal names (LÖMUR, DREKI) unless styling them as Easter eggs for enthusiasts
- Every section should answer: *"Why would I download this right now?"*
